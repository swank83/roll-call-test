    The test, step by step

    1. With the iPad online, open this page in Safari. Wait until Offline files says Ready.
    2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open the app from its new icon. Do the rest of the test inside that icon, because Safari and the home-screen app keep separate data.
    3. Tap Mark present twice, then Undo once. Credits should read 9.
    4. Tap Save backup file and choose Google Drive or Save to Files.
    5. Turn on airplane mode. Close the app fully, then open it from the icon. Connection should say Offline and credits should still read 9.
    6. Tap Erase test data, then Restore from file and pick your backup. Credits should read 9 again.
    7. Leave the iPad alone for a week, then open the app again. Opens should have gone up and credits should still read 9.
    Build v1. Nothing in this test is a real student record.
